# 🪔 Adi's Creation — Diya Collection 2026

> **Lighting up celebrations, one beautiful diya at a time.**

A professional, responsive digital catalogue and enquiry management website developed for **Adi's Creation – Lights n Lamps**, featuring their handcrafted **Diya Collection 2026**.

The website provides customers with an elegant way to explore handcrafted diyas, view product details and pricing, access the official brochure, and submit enquiries. It also includes a secure administrative dashboard for managing customer enquiries and leads.

---

## 🌟 Overview

**Adi's Creation** has been creating beautifully handcrafted diyas for over **15 years**, offering creative, attractive and affordable designs for Diwali and festive celebrations.

The website transforms the brand's 2026 physical brochure into a modern digital experience while preserving its traditional and handcrafted identity.

The platform is designed to be:

- 📱 Mobile-friendly
- 💻 Desktop & laptop responsive
- 🪔 Festive and premium
- ⚡ Fast and user-friendly
- 🔐 Secure
- 📋 Easy to manage
- 🌎 Suitable for customers in India and outside India

## 📋 Enquiry Management

Customers can submit enquiries directly through the website.

### Enquiry Details

The enquiry form can collect:

- Full Name
- Mobile Number
- Email Address
- City
- State
- Country
- Product Interested In
- Quantity
- Delivery Preference
- Preferred Contact Method
- Additional Requirements / Message

Customers can choose between:

- Pickup
- Home Delivery
- International Shipping / Supply
- Not Sure

After submission, the customer receives a confirmation along with an enquiry reference number.

## 🚚 Delivery & Supply

Adi's Creation provides supply:

- 🇮🇳 Across India
- 🌎 Outside India

For international enquiries, shipping and applicable charges are calculated separately based on the destination and requirements.
